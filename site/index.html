How it works

Stop scrolling. It's English.

No screenshots, no share menu, no cropping. You read the way you always do, and the page translates itself whenever you pause.

  1. 1

    Wake Fuki

    Open MangaLens and follow Fuki's two steps: let it float over other apps, then paste a free Gemini key from Google AI Studio (no card needed). Fuki letters a test line to prove it works.

  2. 2

    Tap GO!

    Allow screen capture and a small floating 文A button appears. Tap it to pause or wake MangaLens; long-press it for the quick menu.

  3. 3

    Read like normal

    Brave, Chrome or any reader app. When you stop scrolling, the page goes to the AI in about 150 ms.

  4. 4

    English, in place

    The first line lands about 1.5–2 s later, then the rest stream in one by one. Scroll on and it clears; pages you've read repaint instantly, at no cost.

Features

Lettered like a scanlation, not stuck on like a sticker.

No translucent boxes floating over the art. The original lettering is gone, and English is typeset where it was.

Balloons, wiped clean

Each balloon is cleaned through its own detected shape, then re-lettered in comic type with a letterer's line taper. Joined balloons keep a line each.

Text on the art, too

Narration, signs and sound effects are erased stroke by stroke and re-lettered in their own colours and outline. Big drawn-in sounds keep a small note instead.

Hands-free

Stop and the page translates itself; scroll and the English gets out of your way. Or switch to tap-to-translate and it only works when you ask.

Remembers the story

A glossary, a cast list and the story so far keep names, honorifics and running jokes consistent across pages, chapters and restarts, per series.

Korean · Japanese · Chinese

Webtoons, vertical Japanese, simplified and traditional Chinese, and raws that turn out to be Spanish or English uploads. It reads what's actually on the page.

Auto-scroll

Tap ▼ and the page glides, slowing down while a big balloon passes and hurrying through empty gutters. Awake, it holds at each stop while it translates.

No ghosts

On Android 12 and later, a light veil keeps the original lettering from showing through the English. It lifts the moment MangaLens naps.

Private by design

Screen capture and OCR run on your phone. Pages go only to the AI you chose, with your own key. No account, no ads, no analytics.

New · Manhwa night mode

Dark gaps. The gutters go dark; the art stays exactly as drawn.

A webtoon is art cut by white gutters, and at night the gutters are what hurts. Dark gaps paints them dark and touches nothing else, on any site or app, with or without translation.

  • Only paper is painted. Pale art, a white sky, a balloon: all left as drawn. A gutter has to run out to the edges of the strip.
  • Lettering stays readable. Narration set on the white keeps a margin of white, so black ink is never left on a dark ground.
  • Rides the page as you scroll, and stays off your browser's bars and the site's own header and footer.
  • Never in your finger's way. Long-press 文A → 🌙 Dark gaps to turn it on.

Translation

Pure AI, with your own key.

No machine draft painted first, no offline engine guessing. Each line appears once, in the AI's words, faithful by instruction: every line in full, nothing softened or skipped.

Install

Reading in two minutes.

MangaLens comes as an APK straight from its GitHub releases, signed with the same key every time, so updates install over the top and keep your settings.

  1. 1

    Download

    Get MangaLens.apk on your Android phone or tablet. That link always serves the newest release.

    Every release ships a checksums.txt if you'd like to verify it.

  2. 2

    Open it

    Android asks you to allow installs from your browser: the standard prompt for any app from outside the Play Store.

    Every line of MangaLens is open source.

  3. 3

    Follow Fuki

    Allow "Display over other apps", paste a free Gemini key, and tap GO!. From then on, MangaLens checks for new versions itself and offers them in the app.

"App blocked to protect your device"?

Where Play Protect's enhanced fraud protection is on, Android refuses any downloaded app that declares an accessibility service, which auto-scroll needs. MangaLens's service asks for gestures only: it can't read your screen or anything you type.

The way round: Play Store → your profile picture → Play Protect → ⚙ → turn off Scan apps with Play Protect (and enhanced fraud protection, where shown), install, then turn scanning back on.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

The app is free and open source (MIT), with no account. Translation runs on your own AI key; Google AI Studio's free Gemini tier is plenty for reading.

Does my reading leave my phone?

Screen capture and OCR always run on your device. With Gemini, each page you stop on goes to Google as a 1280 px image (1024 px with Data saver); in text-only mode just the balloon text goes. Either way it goes only to the provider you chose. There's no analytics, no tracking and no account.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything that shows a page: browsers, reader apps, galleries. Private and incognito tabs block screen capture by design (the capture comes out black), so read in a normal tab.

Which languages?

Korean, Japanese (including vertical text) and Chinese (simplified and traditional) into English. Raws that were already translated once, often Spanish or English uploads on aggregator sites, work too.

Overlays don't appear?

Check that "Display over other apps" is allowed and that you're not in a private tab. Without a key nothing is translated, and the status pill says what to add.

Why does the page look darker while it runs?

Since Android 12, an overlay that lets your touches through is drawn at no more than 80% strength, so the original would ghost through the English. A light veil over the page closes that gap. It lifts while MangaLens naps, and Tweaks → No ghosts turns it off.

Does Dark gaps need translation on?

No. Pause translating and the gaps stay dark. It works from the screen capture alone, which is why it works on any site or app. A thin white line next to the art while you scroll is the margin that keeps the shade off the art; it closes when you stop.

What about battery?

Switch to tap-to-translate and capture idles until you tap 文A. Dark gaps only works while the page moves or settles; a still page costs nothing.

Slow or no internet?

Lettering you've already seen repaints from memory at once, and new lines appear one by one as the AI writes them. Data saver shrinks uploads. Offline, nothing new is translated, and the pill tells you why.

How do I update?

MangaLens checks for new versions itself and offers them in the app. You can also install the newest APK over the top at any time; your settings stay. Still on 0.9.1? Follow the one-time update steps.

Respect the creators

MangaLens is a reading-accessibility tool for content you already have access to. When an official English release exists, buy it: translators and artists eat too. ♥
